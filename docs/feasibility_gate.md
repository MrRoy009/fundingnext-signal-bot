## SPEC NOTEs (engineering readings, also in the module docstring)

1. Test windows are UTC dates [first 00:00, last + 1 day 00:00); a candidate belongs to the fold of its signal time T. Every strategy window lies between 08:00 and 20:00 UTC, so a local-date reading assigns the same fold.
2. The 200-candidate threshold is applied unscaled. COMMON 10.1 says "fewer than 200 candidates in total"; amendment A1 pro-rates only the per-fold minimum of 10.4 by the data share, and the 10.4 total of 150 trades, which the 200 protects, is not pro-rated either. Escalated to Quant: if Quant rules that the threshold is pro-rated, the information line under the coverage table gives the pro-rated value.
3. Data share of a fold: trading days with data (cash-open M15 bar present on at least one index) over calendar trading days (union over the ranked indices).
4. In-window cycles are member-cycles (T, index) with the index in G(T), as the strategy's own driver builds G(T); incomplete-group cycles are included, indices outside G(T) are not.
5. The 6.8 flag uses the literal gate (status OK and the V_T condition), so UNAVAILABLE counts as blocked; the share among status-OK cycles is shown and flagged next to it.
6. The per-cycle selection log of COMMON 4.2.8 is written by the CLI to the `--selections` file (gzip text, one line per cycle); see provenance.
